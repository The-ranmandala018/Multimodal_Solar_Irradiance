## Missing or partial evidence
- The CNN+LSTM seed 1–2 and image-only predictions used in the comparisons are in local commit `b025b67`, which is **not yet published**. Until it is, `evaluate_from_publication.py` reports those comparisons as SKIP; the results themselves are in `summary/`.
- Peak RAM and CPU-utilisation time series: not recorded.
- Durations of the geometry, mask and library fits, the Gate 2 rendering and the Phase 1 checks: not recorded; only output times exist.
- The exact revision of `extract_cloud_features.py` used for the reported extraction is not stored as a file; only its sha256 is, in the manifest. The current revision, which adds checkpointing, reproduces the features bit-identically.
- `check_sun_geometry.py` was edited (API update) after its diagnostic run; its output is diagnostic only.
- Phase 1 scripts ran from a temporary folder; the copies in `scripts/lgbm_cloud/phase1/` are identical files, but their console logs were not kept.
